📤 How to upload your certificate to Teams

  1. Press Download my certificate at the end of the module.
  2. Open Teams → the Support Pathway team → Assignments.
  3. Open the assignment for that module.
  4. Tap + Add work → Upload from this device → choose the picture.
  5. Tap Turn in. ✅
